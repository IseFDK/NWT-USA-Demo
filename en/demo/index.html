DEMONSTRATION & DATA NOTES

A working prototype.
An open conversation.

This website demonstrates equipment discovery for a potential US market offering. It is not an official commercial launch or an offer for sale.

Read the references in context.

Business status

NWT Equipment Partners is a temporary concept name for an independent team exploring a potential relationship with New Waste Technology. Official US dealer rights, the selling entity, stock, logistics and support have not been confirmed.

Equipment data

The catalog contains six real model references. Each product page links to its manufacturer and NWT assortment sources, with verification notes. Reference data was reviewed on October 24, 2026 and must be reconfirmed for the actual machine.

Unclear throughput, engine, compliance and transport claims are deliberately left open. Rounded US customary conversions are shown alongside the metric source values. No US short-ton capacity is claimed.

Finder logic

A known process narrows the shortlist. Documented material and mobility matches improve ordering. Missing or unconfirmed data remains eligible and appears under details to confirm. Operating frequency is carried into the inquiry; it does not assert a capacity match.

Media and documents

The project owner reported NWT permission to use its website catalogue for this demo and approved its inclusion after manufacturer origins were identified. The photos are reference configurations, not our projects or US inventory. Manufacturer licensing scope still needs confirmation before a commercial launch. Original diagrams are labeled. Official videos use click-to-load embeds; PDFs remain on manufacturer websites.

Demonstration contacts

Verified US sales contacts have not been provided, so external contact actions are disabled. Copy inquiry works locally. The website does not send messages or save leads.

Search and access

Every page is marked noindex, nofollow, noarchive and nosnippet. These are search directives, not access protection. The source repository is intended to remain private; any hosted demo link may be publicly accessible.
