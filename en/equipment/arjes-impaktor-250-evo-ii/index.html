Overview

The EVO II combines a twin-shaft processing system with a hopper and tracked chassis. Its published shaft dimensions and speed are reference values; the shaft configuration must be selected around the actual material stream.

Design & operation

Changing the working unit

The cassette groups the shafts into a replaceable unit. Shaft and screen-basket selection still depends on the material. [A1][A2]

Adjusting the working gap

The manufacturer describes removable waveform plates for changing the gap. This is not a guaranteed finished-particle specification. [A1]

Configuration notes & unresolved data

  • The English page lists TAD 551 VE / Stage IIIA for ROW; the manufacturer flyer lists TAD 581 VE / EU Stage V. These are separate references, not one engine specification.
  • The web page gives wood-waste throughput as 10 metric tonnes/h; the flyer gives 10–20. Wood throughput is withheld pending shaft and feed clarification.
  • The manufacturer describes the overbelt magnet as both included and optional. Magnet and sprinkler fitment must be confirmed for the actual unit.
  • Source horsepower labels differ (HP / PS). This page uses kW and independently calculated US mechanical hp; the source labels are not treated as equivalent.

Still to confirm

  • Engine displacement and exact gearbox model are not confirmed.
  • US engine approval and the supplied shaft, magnet, sprinkler and basket configuration require confirmation.
