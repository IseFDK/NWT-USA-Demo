Overview

The K-DDS 5012 uses a disc-screen system to size previously shredded material and remove fines. The manufacturer describes two to three fractions; unverified screenbox, mass and capacity figures are deliberately left open.

Design & operation

How the discs separate material

The flat-profile disc system moves material along the shafts while lifting and lowering it. The published purpose is sizing shredded feed and removing fines. [D1][D2]

Published applications

Wood & biomassCompostMunicipal / mixed wasteConstruction & demolition

Material tags are broad references. Feed size, contamination, moisture and machine configuration can change suitability.

Machine configuration

Tracked disc screener.

Light construction and demolition waste is described; this does not establish suitability for heavy unprocessed demolition debris.

Engine & Key Components
