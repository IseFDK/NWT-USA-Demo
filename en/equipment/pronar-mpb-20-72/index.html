Overview

The base MPB 20.72 pairs a rotating screening drum with a feed hopper and a two-axle semi-trailer chassis. Drum geometry, sieve area and drum speed are listed below for this reference configuration.

Design & operation

Access around the drive

The rotating engine frame and opening guards expose the drive unit, hopper and hydraulics for service access. [P1]
